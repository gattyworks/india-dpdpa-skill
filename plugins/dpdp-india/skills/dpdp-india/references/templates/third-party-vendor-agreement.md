# Third-Party Vendor Agreement

_Purpose: commercial contract layer (broader than a pure DPA) governing any vendor that receives or touches personal data, with data-protection obligations embedded. DPDP mapping: S.8(2) (processing via contract), S.8(5) (reasonable security safeguards). Source: https://www.dpdpa.com/templates/thirdpartyvendoragreementtemplate.html. Paraphrased structure. Last verified: 2026-06-15._

**When required**
When sharing personal data with any third-party vendor (resellers, integrators, support providers) — used where a full DPA is paired with or folded into a master services agreement. The Fiduciary stays accountable for the vendor's processing.

**Required clauses checklist** (paraphrased)
- Definitions and scope of services.
- Vendor obligations: process per company instructions and applicable law; personnel confidentiality; security measures including encryption and access control; breach reporting within set timeframes; prior approval before engaging sub-processors; assistance with rights requests and assessments; return/secure deletion at end of term.
- Company (Fiduciary) obligations: lawful instructions, audit rights.
- Confidentiality undertakings.
- Indemnification and liability.
- Cross-border transfer compliance (S.16) where applicable.
- Term and termination.
- Governing law (India) and dispute resolution.
- Miscellaneous (amendments, notices).

**Audit signals**
- Vendor contracts include the data-protection clause set above (not just commercial terms).
- A vendor register listing each vendor, data shared, and contract status.
- Security obligations are verifiable (encryption in transit/at rest, least-privilege access).
- Breach-reporting timeframe flows up to the Fiduciary's own breach process.
- Offboarding checklist confirms data return/deletion.
