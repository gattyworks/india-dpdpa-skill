# Consent & Notice (4-7) + Consent Manager

## 4 - Grounds for processing personal data
A person may process a Data Principal's personal data only in accordance with the Act and **for a lawful purpose** that is either:
- (a) one **for which the Data Principal has given her consent**; or
- (b) for **certain legitimate uses** (the 7 list).

> **4(2)** "lawful purpose" means any purpose which is not expressly forbidden by law.

There is **no** "legitimate interests" balancing test as in GDPR - the non-consent grounds are the closed 7 list only.

## 5 - Notice (required contents)
Every consent request under 6 must be **accompanied or preceded by a notice** from the Data Fiduciary informing the Data Principal of:
1. **(i)** the personal data and the **purpose** for which it is proposed to be processed;
2. **(ii)** the **manner of exercising her rights** under 6(4) (consent withdrawal) and 13 (grievance redressal);
3. **(iii)** the **manner of making a complaint to the Board**.

Additional notice rules:
- **5(2)** - For consent obtained **before** commencement of the Act, the fiduciary must, as soon as reasonably practicable, give a notice covering the same items; it may continue processing until consent is withdrawn.
- **5(3)** - Must offer the notice in **English or any language in the Eighth Schedule** to the Constitution.
- Exact "manner" of the notice is **to be prescribed** by rules (verify final rules).

## 6 - Consent
> **6(1)** Consent "shall be **free, specific, informed, unconditional and unambiguous with a clear affirmative action**, and shall signify an agreement to the processing of her personal data for the specified purpose and be limited to such personal data as is necessary for such specified purpose."

Key requirements:
- **6(1) - data minimisation by purpose:** consent is limited to data **necessary** for the specified purpose. (Illustration: a telemedicine app cannot bundle phone-contact-list access into consent for telemedicine.)
- **6(2) - infringing parts are invalid:** any part of consent that infringes the Act/rules/other law is invalid to that extent (e.g. a clause waiving the right to complain to the Board).
- **6(3) - clear plain language**, option to access the request in English / an Eighth-Schedule language, and **contact details of the DPO** (where applicable) or other authorised contact.
- **6(4) - withdrawal:** right to withdraw consent at any time, "with the **ease of doing so being comparable to the ease with which such consent was given**."
- **6(5) - consequences of withdrawal** are borne by the Data Principal; withdrawal does not retroactively invalidate prior lawful processing.
- **6(6) - on withdrawal**, the fiduciary must within a reasonable time **cease, and cause its processors to cease**, processing - unless retention/processing is otherwise required or authorised by law.
- **6(7)-(8) - Consent Manager:** the Data Principal may give/manage/review/withdraw consent **through a Consent Manager**, who is accountable to the Data Principal.
- **6(9) - Consent Manager registration** with the Board, subject to prescribed conditions.
- **6(10) - burden of proof:** in any proceeding, the **Data Fiduciary must prove** that a valid notice was given and valid consent obtained.

## 7 - Certain legitimate uses (the full list)
A Data Fiduciary may process personal data **without fresh consent** for:
- **(a)** the **specified purpose for which the Data Principal voluntarily provided** her data and has not indicated she does not consent.
- **(b)** the **State / its instrumentalities** to provide a subsidy, benefit, service, certificate, licence or permit (as prescribed), where she previously consented or the data is in a notified State database.
- **(c)** performance by the **State** of any legal function, or in the interest of **sovereignty/integrity of India or security of the State**.
- **(d)** fulfilling any **legal obligation to disclose** information to the State.
- **(e)** compliance with any **judgment/decree/order** (Indian, or foreign orders on contractual/civil claims).
- **(f)** responding to a **medical emergency** (threat to life/immediate threat to health) of the Principal or any individual.
- **(g)** **medical treatment / health services** during an epidemic, disease outbreak, or threat to public health.
- **(h)** ensuring **safety / assistance during a disaster** or breakdown of public order. ("disaster" per 2(d) of the Disaster Management Act, 2005.)
- **(i)** **employment** purposes or safeguarding the employer from loss/liability (corporate espionage prevention, trade-secret/IP/classified-info confidentiality, or providing a service/benefit sought by an employee Data Principal).

## Consent Manager role (summary)
- 2(g): a person **registered with the Board** acting as a **single point of contact** to let a Data Principal give, manage, review and withdraw consent via an **accessible, transparent and interoperable** platform.
- Accountable to the Data Principal and acts on her behalf (6(8)); registered subject to prescribed technical/operational/financial conditions (6(9)).

## Actionable checklist - compliant consent/notice flow

**Notice (5)**
- [ ] Notice is shown **before or with** every consent request.
- [ ] Lists the **specific personal data** collected and the **specific purpose**.
- [ ] Explains **how to withdraw consent** (6(4)) and **how to raise a grievance** (13).
- [ ] Explains **how to complain to the Data Protection Board**.
- [ ] Available in **English + Eighth-Schedule languages**; clear, plain language.
- [ ] Legacy/pre-Act consents: retro-notice issued as soon as practicable (5(2)).

**Consent (6)**
- [ ] Captured via **clear affirmative action** (no pre-ticked boxes, no bundling).
- [ ] **Itemised / granular** per purpose; only data **necessary** for each purpose.
- [ ] **No conditional bundling** (consent to unrelated processing not forced as a precondition).
- [ ] No invalid clauses (e.g. waivers of statutory rights).
- [ ] **DPO / authorised contact** details shown (where applicable).
- [ ] **Withdrawal is as easy as granting** - one-click/equivalent, same channel.
- [ ] On withdrawal: processing **stops** at the fiduciary **and all processors** within a reasonable time.
- [ ] **Consent records retained** (timestamp, version of notice, scope) to discharge the 6(10) burden of proof.
- [ ] **Consent Manager** integration supported where used (give/manage/review/withdraw).

**Legitimate uses (7)**
- [ ] Each non-consent processing activity maps to a **specific 7 clause** - documented.
- [ ] Voluntarily-provided-data (7(a)) processing stops when the Principal signals she no longer wants it.
