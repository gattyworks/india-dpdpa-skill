# Indian legal context for an app audit

**Primary sources:** DPDP Act 2023 and commencement notification; [CERT-In directions page](https://cert-in.org.in/Directions70B.jsp); [RBI payment-data storage direction](https://rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=11244); Supreme Court judgment records for *Justice K.S. Puttaswamy (Retd.) v. Union of India*.

**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-15. Check the current sector rules for each audit.

## Constitutional context

The nine-judge Supreme Court bench in *Puttaswamy* recognised privacy as a fundamental right. Use
the judgment as constitutional context. Do not use it as a substitute for a DPDPA section or rule.

## IT Act and SPDI transition

Section 44(2) of DPDPA omits 43A of the Information Technology Act, 2000. Section 44(2) is in the
eighteen-month phase under G.S.R. 843(E). Check the current status of 43A, the SPDI Rules 2011, and
any replacement instrument on the audit date.

Do not assume DPDPA has already displaced every existing privacy or security duty.

## CERT-In incident reporting

The CERT-In directions require covered entities to report listed cyber incidents within six hours
of noticing the incident or being informed of it. They also include clock, log, and specified
provider-record duties.

This is a separate trigger from DPDPA's personal-data-breach rule. A single event can require both
tracks. Confirm that the event and organisation fall within the current CERT-In directions.

## Payment-data localisation

The RBI's 6 April 2018 direction requires covered payment system providers to store the full
payment-system data in India. It permits foreign-leg data to be stored abroad when required.

Apply the RBI direction only to covered entities and data. Check later RBI directions and FAQs for
the product's exact role.

## Other sector rules

Securities, insurance, telecom, health, banking, and government systems can have additional data,
security, retention, and incident duties. Identify the regulator and current instrument before
using DPDPA as the full compliance boundary.

## Board status and precedent

The Data Protection Board was established in November 2025. MeitY advertised Chairperson and Member
roles in May 2026. An official appointment notice was not located in the sources reviewed on
2026-08-15.

Do not claim mature DPDPA case law or Board enforcement practice without a current primary source.
Base the engineering audit on the Act, final Rules, corrigendum, commencement notification, and
current official orders.
