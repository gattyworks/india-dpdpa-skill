# Employee Privacy / Data Protection Policy

_Purpose: govern handling of employee personal data across the employment lifecycle. DPDP mapping: S.7(i) (specified employment-related legitimate uses), S.8 (Fiduciary obligations). Source: https://www.dpdpa.com/templates/employeedataprotectionpolicytemplate.html. Paraphrased structure. Third-party structure and statutory mapping reviewed 2026-08-15._

**When required**
Readiness artifact for an organization processing employee, candidate, or contractor personal
data in India. S.7(i) covers specified employment-related uses; it is not a blanket basis for all
workforce processing. Map each purpose separately and apply the future-phase S.8 obligations when
they commence.

**Required clauses checklist** (paraphrased)
- Introduction establishing the compliance basis.
- Objectives and scope (who and what data is covered).
- Definitions (personal data, DPO, processing).
- Data processing principles (lawfulness, purpose limitation, minimization, accuracy, storage limitation, security).
- Categories of employee data collected (identification, financial/payroll, health, biometric where used).
- Permitted uses of employee data (payroll, benefits, performance, legal compliance).
- Security safeguards and access controls.
- Retention and deletion of employee records.
- Employee rights and how to exercise them; grievance contact.
- DPO / contact information.

**Audit signals**
- An employee-facing privacy notice in the HR system / onboarding pack.
- Access controls restricting HR data to need-to-know roles.
- Special handling for sensitive categories (health, biometric attendance).
- Retention rules for ex-employee records and a deletion process.
- A route for employees to raise data grievances internally.
