# Cross-Border Data Transfer Agreement

_Purpose: document controls for transferring personal data from India to a foreign recipient. DPDP mapping: S.16 (government restrictions on transfer outside India); Rule 15 (requirements that the Central Government may specify). Source: https://www.dpdpa.com/templates/crossborderdatatransferagreementtemplate.html. Paraphrased structure. Third-party structure and statutory mapping reviewed 2026-08-15._

**When required**
Future-phase readiness artifact as of 2026-08-15. Use it when personal data of Indian principals
is transferred outside India, including offshore hosting, overseas affiliates, and foreign
sub-processors. Section 16 allows the Central Government to restrict transfers by notification.
Rule 15 allows it to specify requirements for making data available to a foreign state or an
entity under its control. Sectoral localization rules may be stricter. No general DPDPA transfer
notification or standardized contractual-clause regime was located in the official source set as
of the review date.

**Required clauses checklist** (paraphrased)
- Definitions, including any destination or access restriction that applies when the agreement is signed.
- Scope and purpose: data categories and transfer objectives.
- Data importer obligations: equivalent protection, security measures, breach notification.
- Data exporter obligations: provide lawful basis, retain audit rights.
- Cross-border controls: destination review, access-risk assessment, and any requirements in a current government notification.
- Data Principal rights facilitation across borders (access, correction, deletion).
- Indemnity and liability (including caps).
- Term, termination, and data return/deletion.
- Governing law (India) and arbitration.
- Amendment and notice provisions.

**Audit signals**
- An inventory of data flows leaving India (which datasets, to which countries/providers).
- A transfer agreement or equivalent contractual controls on file for each offshore recipient. SCCs may be used as a risk control; DPDPA does not currently prescribe a standard SCC form.
- A documented check of current DPDPA notifications and any sector-specific destination or localization limits.
- Sectoral localization requirements (e.g., RBI payment data) honored where applicable.
- Importer breach-notification path connects back to the exporter's process.
