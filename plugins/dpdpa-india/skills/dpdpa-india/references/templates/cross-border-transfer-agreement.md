# Cross-Border Data Transfer Agreement

_Purpose: set lawful conditions for transferring personal data from India to a foreign recipient. DPDP mapping: S.16 (restriction on transfer outside India; government may notify restricted countries); Rule 15 (transfer outside the territory of India). Source: https://www.dpdpa.com/templates/crossborderdatatransferagreementtemplate.html. Paraphrased structure. Last verified: 2026-06-15._

**When required**
Whenever personal data of Indian principals is transferred outside India (offshore hosting, overseas affiliates, foreign sub-processors). S.16 permits transfer except to countries restricted by government notification; sectoral laws imposing stricter localization continue to apply. The agreement documents safeguards for the importer.

**Required clauses checklist** (paraphrased)
- Definitions, including "notified/restricted countries."
- Scope and purpose: data categories and transfer objectives.
- Data importer obligations: equivalent protection, security measures, breach notification.
- Data exporter obligations: provide lawful basis, retain audit rights.
- Cross-border transfer requirements: standard contractual clauses, transfer risk assessment, check against restricted-country list.
- Data Principal rights facilitation across borders (access, correction, deletion).
- Indemnity and liability (including caps).
- Term, termination, and data return/deletion.
- Governing law (India) and arbitration.
- Amendment and notice provisions.

**Audit signals**
- An inventory of data flows leaving India (which datasets, to which countries/providers).
- A transfer agreement / SCCs on file for each offshore recipient.
- A check that destinations are not on any government-restricted list.
- Sectoral localization requirements (e.g., RBI payment data) honored where applicable.
- Importer breach-notification path connects back to the exporter's process.
