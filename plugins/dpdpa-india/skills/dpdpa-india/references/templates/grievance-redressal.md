# Grievance Redressal

_Purpose: a readily available channel and defined timeline for Data Principals to complain to the Data Fiduciary before escalating to the Data Protection Board. DPDP mapping: S.13 (right of grievance redressal), S.8(10) (Fiduciary must publish DPO/contact for grievance), S.5/S.6 (the response duty exists regardless of basis). Source structure informed by https://www.dpdpa.com/templates/datasubjectrightsrequestformtemplate.html. Paraphrased structure. Last verified: 2026-06-15._

**When required**
All Data Fiduciaries. S.13 gives every principal the right to grievance redressal directly with the Fiduciary (or its Consent Manager), and S.8(10) requires publishing the contact details of the DPO or a responsible person. The principal must exhaust this channel before approaching the Board.

**Required clauses checklist** (paraphrased)
- Named grievance officer / DPO with reachable contact details.
- Clear intake channel (form, email, portal).
- Defined response timeline for resolving grievances (publish the period the Fiduciary commits to).
- Categories of grievances handled (data accuracy, misuse, consent, rights non-fulfilment).
- Acknowledgement and tracking-reference issuance.
- Escalation path to the Data Protection Board if unresolved.
- Recordkeeping of grievances and outcomes.

**Audit signals**
- Published DPO / grievance-officer contact on the site/app (footer, privacy policy, contact page).
- A grievance intake mechanism that works (form submits, email monitored).
- A complaints log/queue with timestamps and resolution status against a stated SLA.
- A documented internal procedure for routing and resolving grievances.
- Notice to principals about escalation to the Board.
