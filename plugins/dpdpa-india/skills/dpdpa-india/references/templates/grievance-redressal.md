# Grievance Redressal

_Purpose: a readily available channel and defined timeline for Data Principals to complain to the Data Fiduciary before escalating to the Data Protection Board. DPDP mapping: S.13 (right of grievance redressal), S.8(10) (Fiduciary must publish DPO/contact for grievance), Rule 14. Source structure informed by https://www.dpdpa.com/templates/datasubjectrightsrequestformtemplate.html. Paraphrased structure. Third-party structure and statutory mapping reviewed 2026-08-15._

**When required**
Future-phase readiness artifact as of 2026-08-15. Once the relevant provisions commence, S.13
gives a Data Principal the right to grievance redressal directly with the Fiduciary or Consent
Manager, and S.8(10) requires publishing a business contact for rights questions. The principal
must exhaust this channel before approaching the Board.

**Required clauses checklist** (paraphrased)
- Named grievance officer / DPO with reachable contact details.
- Clear intake channel (form, email, portal).
- Defined response timeline for resolving grievances. Rule 14 requires a published period not exceeding 90 days.
- Categories of grievances handled (data accuracy, misuse, consent, rights non-fulfilment).
- Acknowledgement and tracking-reference issuance.
- Escalation path to the Data Protection Board if unresolved.
- Recordkeeping of grievances and outcomes.

**Audit signals**
- Published DPO / grievance-officer contact on the site/app (footer, privacy policy, contact page).
- A grievance intake mechanism that works (form submits, email monitored).
- A complaints log/queue with timestamps and resolution status against a stated SLA.
- A documented internal procedure for routing and resolving grievances.
- Notice to principals about escalation to the Board.
